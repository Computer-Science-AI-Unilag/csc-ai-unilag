# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want this learning archive to be respectful and welcoming for students, instructors, and contributors of every background and experience level.

## Expected behaviour

- Be constructive, patient, and academically honest.
- Give feedback about work, not people.
- Respect privacy, consent, and intellectual-property rights.
- Credit sources and contributors accurately.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, plagiarism, sharing private information, abusive language, or publishing content without permission is unacceptable.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to a maintainer through GitHub. Do not put sensitive details in a public issue. Maintainers may remove content or limit participation where appropriate.

# CSE AI UNILAG

## Artificial Intelligence Learning, Research & Open Educational Resources

CSE AI UNILAG is a lecturer-led, community-maintained learning archive for Artificial Intelligence education at the University of Lagos, Nigeria. It preserves approved course documentation, practical resources, recordings, and student-project references across academic sessions. It is not presented as an official University of Lagos programme or endorsement.

## Vision and mission

We make AI teaching easier to revisit, contribute to, and improve. Materials stay organised by academic year while the learning path remains clear for students meeting AI formally for the first time.

## What this repository contains

- Academic-year archives and course documentation
- Lecture notes, slides, labs, exercises, and recording indexes
- Student project documentation and approved external links
- Student guides for Python, GitHub, and Google Colab
- Templates and contribution workflows

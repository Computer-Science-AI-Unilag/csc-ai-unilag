## Overview

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the field of designing systems that can perceive information, reason or learn from it, and act toward a goal. AI is not one technology: a navigation app choosing a route, a phone unlocking with a face, and a program recommending a song use different methods to make useful decisions.

## Learning objectives

Students should be able to:

- describe AI in plain language and explain why it matters;
- distinguish AI from the narrower field of machine learning;
- identify an intelligent agent, its environment, sensors, actuators, and performance measure;
- recognise that responsible AI involves people, context, and possible harms.

## Prerequisites

No prior AI course is required. Curiosity, clear reasoning, and basic familiarity with software are enough.

## Important concepts

- **Artificial Intelligence:** systems designed for perception, reasoning, learning, language, or decision-making.
- **Machine Learning:** methods that improve a task from data; one approach within AI.
- **Intelligent agent:** an entity that observes an environment and chooses actions toward a goal.
- **PEAS:** Performance measure, Environment, Actuators, and Sensors.

## Practical demonstration

Imagine a traffic-light assistant at a busy Lagos junction. Its sensors might count vehicles and pedestrians; its actuators switch lights; its performance measure favours safety and lower waiting time. What happens if a sensor fails or the measure ignores pedestrians?

# Week 01 Sample Assignment: AI Around Us

**Status:** Sample educational exercise, pending lecturer adoption.
**Submission details:** To be confirmed by the lecturer.

## Task

1. Identify three real-world AI applications.
2. Explain how each system perceives or interacts with its environment.
3. Choose one and outline its PEAS: performance measure, environment, actuators, and sensors.

## Guidance

Use examples you can explain clearly. State assumptions and discuss one ethical concern for the chosen system.

## Suggested format

Write 500–800 words or create an equivalent labelled diagram/table. Cite external sources. Do not include personal data or screenshots containing private information.

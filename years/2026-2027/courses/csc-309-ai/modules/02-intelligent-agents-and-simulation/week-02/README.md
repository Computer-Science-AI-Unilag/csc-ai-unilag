## Overview

Week 02 develops the intelligent-agent view of AI: perception, action, feedback, policies, PEAS, internal state, and the relationship between an architecture, a policy, and an algorithm. The supporting material introduces reflex, model-based, goal-based, and utility-based agents alongside BFS, uniform-cost search, and A*. The practical labs make those concepts observable through particle/ant-foraging and Pacman search simulations.

## Important concepts

- **Agent:** an entity that observes an environment through sensors and acts through actuators.
- **Policy:** the rule or procedure that selects an action from available information.
- **PEAS:** Performance measure, Environment, Actuators, and Sensors.
- **Agent architecture:** the organisation of perception, memory, decision-making, and action.

# Week 01: Introduction to Artificial Intelligence

**Academic session:** 2026/2027
**Course:** CSC 309: Artificial Intelligence
**Module:** Foundations and Philosophy of AI
**Lecturer:** Dr. Roselyn Isimeto and Bunmi Akinremi
**Date:** Not published
**Status:** Lecture and practical materials archived

## Overview

Week 01 introduces Artificial Intelligence from historical, functional, and rational-agent perspectives. The teaching materials cover major AI eras, goals, techniques, classifications, applications, and ethical questions. The practical session uses face recognition to connect these ideas to machine-learning evaluation and responsible deployment.

## Learning objectives

Students should be able to define AI, describe major historical milestones, distinguish AI approaches and classifications, identify core AI techniques and applications, and discuss social and ethical implications.

## Practical class: Face recognition

The [face-recognition lab notebook](practical/face-recognition-lab.ipynb) is the practical-class Colab resource. It guides students through image representation, eigenfaces with nearest-neighbour classification, hyperparameter effects, accuracy, false-accept and false-reject rates, and ethics.

Open the [practical guide](practical/README.md) before running the notebook.

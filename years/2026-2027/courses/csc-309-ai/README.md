# CSC 309: Artificial Intelligence

**Academic session:** [2026/2027](../../README.md)
**Course code:** CSC 309
**Status:** Active course archive

## Overview

CSC 309 introduces Artificial Intelligence through foundations and philosophy, intelligent agents and simulation, problem solving, knowledge representation, expert systems, and modern AI applications. This archive preserves the lecture and practical materials supplied for the 2026/2027 session.

## Prerequisites

Basic programming, problem-solving, and introductory discrete-mathematics ideas are helpful. See the [Python setup guide](../../../../resources/python-setup.md).

## Learning outcomes

Students work with foundational AI concepts, rational agents, PEAS analysis, search and decision-making ideas, practical model evaluation, and responsible AI questions.
